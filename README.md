# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis is a data analytics project focused on understanding customer purchasing patterns, preferences, and shopping trends. The project involves data cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), SQL analysis, and Power BI dashboard development to generate meaningful business insights.

The main objective is to analyze customer behavior, identify revenue trends, and understand the factors that influence purchasing decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior based on gender, age, and product category.
- Identify the highest revenue-generating product categories.
- Understand the impact of discounts and promotional codes on purchases.
- Analyze customer subscription status and purchasing frequency.
- Evaluate customer ratings and preferred payment methods.
- Generate actionable insights to support business decisions.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer shopping information with the following attributes:

- Customer_ID
- Age
- Gender
- Item_Purchased
- Category
- Purchase_Amount_USD
- Location
- Size
- Color
- Season
- Review_Rating
- Subscription_Status
- Shipping_Type
- Discount_Applied
- Promo_Code_Used
- Previous_Purchases
- Payment_Method
- Frequency_of_Purchases

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

EDA is performed to understand the structure, quality, distribution, and relationships within the dataset.

### 1. Data Understanding

- Loaded the dataset into Python using Pandas.
- Examined the number of rows and columns.
- Reviewed column names and data types.
- Used descriptive statistics to understand numerical variables.

### 2. Data Cleaning

- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
- Standardized column names for consistency.
- Verified data types and corrected them where necessary.
- Checked numerical columns for invalid or inconsistent values.
- Prepared the dataset for further analysis.

### 3. Univariate Analysis

Analyzed individual variables to understand customer demographics and shopping preferences.

- Customer age distribution
- Gender distribution
- Purchase amount distribution
- Product category distribution
- Customer review ratings
- Preferred payment methods

### 4. Bivariate Analysis

Explored relationships between two variables to identify shopping patterns.

- Gender vs. Purchase Amount
- Age vs. Purchase Amount
- Product Category vs. Revenue
- Subscription Status vs. Purchase Behavior
- Discount Usage vs. Purchase Amount
- Review Rating vs. Product Category

### 5. Customer Behavior Analysis

- Examined customer purchasing frequency.
- Analyzed previous purchases to understand customer activity.
- Compared subscriber and non-subscriber shopping behavior.
- Identified popular product categories and shopping preferences.
- Evaluated the use of discounts and promotional codes.

### 6. Revenue Analysis

- Calculated total revenue generated from purchases.
- Compared revenue across genders and product categories.
- Identified high-performing products and locations.
- Examined seasonal shopping trends.
- Analyzed payment methods and shipping preferences.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?
2. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
3. Which age groups contribute most to sales?
4. How does subscription status relate to customer purchasing behavior?
5. Do discounts and promotional codes influence purchase amounts?
6. Which locations contribute the most revenue?

9. What are the seasonal shopping trends?
10. Which customer segments could be targeted to improve sales?

## Business Insights and Recommendations

The following insights should be finalized after analyzing the actual dataset:

- **Customer Segmentation:** Identify important customer groups based on age, gender, and purchase behavior.
- **Product Performance:** Focus on product categories that contribute significantly to total revenue.
- **Customer Retention:** Develop strategies to encourage repeat purchases and improve customer loyalty.
- **Promotional Strategy:** Evaluate discount and promotional code usage before designing new campaigns.
- **Customer Experience:** Use review ratings and shopping preferences to identify opportunities for improvement.
- **Sales Optimization:** Use seasonal and location-based trends to support inventory and marketing decisions.
